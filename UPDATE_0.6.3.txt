UPGRADE CROSSROADS 0.6.3 - ONE CONTINUOUS JOURNAL PANORAMA

Both panes now share one original wide painting. Mountains, valley and terrain
continue across the middle instead of repeating as a second version. A pale
parchment wash over the right side keeps its dark text readable.

GAME INSTALL
Close WoW, extract UpgradeCrossroads-v0.6.3-forever.zip and replace the existing
UpgradeCrossroads folder in Interface/AddOns. Keep your WTF/SavedVariables.
Restart the client so the new texture filename is available. /uc status should
say v0.6.3. Open /uc and inspect the continuous scene across both journal panes.

SOURCE UPDATE
Extract UpgradeCrossroads-v0.6.3-source-update.zip into C:\Dev\UpgradeCrossroads,
replacing its files. The ZIP includes the complete changes from public v0.5.3
through v0.6.3, including Core/Journal.lua, UI/Journal.lua and the TOC. It can be
applied over v0.5.3, v0.6.0, v0.6.1 or v0.6.2. The install ZIP belongs in the
game; the source update belongs in your project.

If you previously copied the 0.6.1/0.6.2 files, remove the two obsolete files
UpgradeCrossroads/Media/JournalLandscape.tga and
UpgradeCrossroads/Media/JournalParchment.tga from your project and game addon
folder. They are no longer used. Keep the original PNG masters under art/.

All prior comparison, hunts/notes, tracker, map, chat, filters, custom weights,
appearance and resizing features remain. Data is still the October 13 snapshot.
Offline checks and previews are not a live WoW test. No release tag or CurseForge
upload has been created by this update.
